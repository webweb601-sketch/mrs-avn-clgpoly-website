ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Learning today.
Building tomorrow.

MRS. AVN Polytechnic College is located in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India.

Polytechnic education connects foundational knowledge with applied technical skills. Through classroom learning, practical exercises, workshops and project work, diploma students can explore engineering concepts and develop confidence in solving real-world problems.

This website introduces the college, its engineering branches, student activities and learning resources. Official institutional details are marked for confirmation where required.
